### Spec audit, part 3 (2026-10-31)

The spec's prose says "the current compiler" where it said "main" for the compiler, so "main" now
means only the entry-point function. Normative sentences keep their text and versions.

Each rationale explains its entry's current version: what earlier versions said, and why they changed, were moved to a non-normative `## Version history` appendix at the end of ten chapters
(MAINTAINERS §2.1 states the convention).

OWN-6 and OWN-7, and PAT-5, PAT-8 and PAT-10, stay separate entries (their kinds, codes or statuses differ, so a merge would change what a diagnostic names); each rationale now says how it divides the ground with the others and which code reports the shared case.

The qualification rule of SYNTAX_FREEZE_REVIEW §1.1 is absorbed into SYN-10's rationale as current behaviour (the case of a path's first letter decides between a type member and a module member, and a module path has one level), with two open questions: OPEN-151 (module paths of more than one level, OD151) and OPEN-152 (whether the first letter decides, OD152).

E0310's message, hint and ledger row no longer name `fn main() -> Int`: the entry point takes no parameters, and the hint says to declare `main` with none.
