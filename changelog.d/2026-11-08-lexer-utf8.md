The settled slice-0 lexer components now have independent declarative judgments in
`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Surface0.lean` and total implementations with soundness and completeness
proofs in `Concrete/Lex.lean`: Table 3-7 UTF-8, NUL and BOM refusal, ASCII identifier characters,
and LF/CR/EOF line comments. Kernel-checked examples cover valid encodings and each refusal;
errors carry byte offsets and internal reason constructors. The first-error-offset theorem is proved against an independent prefix-continuation
judgment. No token rules or user-visible spellings are introduced, and P_lex
remains open for the whole lexer.

Review revision: source policy is owner-approved language list item 30 (OPEN-1),
not an undecided rule. Surface0 now cites that authority, distinguishes raw source
from Q4.2 text values, and carries the standard PROPOSED header. Scalar range
checks reflect Borrow.isScalar rather than defining a second UTF-8 validity rule.
Scalar and determinism proofs live in ConcreteSpec.Proofs.Surface0. Comment
skipping returns only the remaining suffix, without allocating discarded trivia.
Packaged success iff and error-refusal lemmas are provided; source completeness
requires just the decoding and its scalar restrictions. Recursion-depth overrides
are removed. All compiler sources are scanned for trust violations, and lexer
correspondence theorems are registered in the trusted-base inventory.

The error-path revision factors a proof-carrying single-scalar step and a shared
accumulator loop for UTF-8 decoding and source checking. Each lead/continuation
check precedes inspecting the next byte; NUL/BOM checks precede the tail call.
The loop uses constant execution stack space, with erased termination proofs.
`invalidContinuation` now means a non-continuation in a required continuation slot;
`unexpectedContinuation` means a lone continuation used as a lead.

`ScalarPrefix` means that a byte prefix has a well-formed scalar continuation.
`ScalarFailure` requires an unfinished viable prefix followed by an impossible
byte, or EOF; `FirstError` adds the preceding complete scalars. Its offset is
proved unique independently of the implementation. `decode_error_iff` proves
both directions, including the exact offset; `checkSource_error_first` proves
source refusals occur at the first invalid or forbidden scalar. NUL/BOM offsets
remain scalar-start offsets. The UTF-8 erasure lemmas connect scalar/value decoding
to Borrow's authoritative text UTF-8 judgment.

API migration for stacked branches: `CommentBody` drops its discarded-body argument;
`LineComment` and `lineComment` return the remaining scalar suffix, and `scanLine`
returns that suffix instead of a body/suffix pair. `checkSource_complete` takes the
decoding and its pointwise restrictions instead of a redundant `SourceOk` witness.
`badSequence`, `prepend`, `prepend_ok`, `sourcePrepend`, and `sourcePrepend_ok` are
removed by the shared-step/loop design. `Concrete.Lex.encodes_scalar` and
`Concrete.Lex.decodes_unique` remain available as aliases of the spec proofs.
No other existing public names are renamed. Compiler examples now join the trust
gate's named-example axiom audit as well as its source and whole-environment scans.
