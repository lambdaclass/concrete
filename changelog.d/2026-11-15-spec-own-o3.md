### F16: scoped ownership protocols and loan histories

Add O3 Laws for downward reborrow restoration and upward refusal, deferred call
lifetime, move/reinitialization, one-step retirement and finite loan histories.
Use Exec's existing dispatcher and fueled driver for deferred execution; substitute
the lifecycle through the same operands throughout each run. Keep the original
caller loans in the postcondition as well as ending the deferred call's own loan.
Check every callee prefix, detecting a premature end even when endpoints agree.

Add populated instances and one-rule body mutants for upward lending, caller-parent
loss, retained ownership, reissued owner/reborrow identities, rewound ending supply,
premature adoption-time release, leaked call loans and implicit ending of unrelated
caller loans. Histories contain multiple successful actions, an ending and a fresh reborrow. Reuse O1 fixtures and
its private slot-uniqueness helper; place the move protocol beside that helper
without a second definition. O1/O2 retain the restated per-operation and erasure
facts; record their disposition in the new module rather than duplicating declarations or executable policies.
