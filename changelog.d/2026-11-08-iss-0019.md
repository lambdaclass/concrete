### Review-provenance gap filed as ISS-0019 (2026-11-08)

The issue ledger records a high-severity review-provenance gap: `train.sh` accepts a clean review note without checking that its base covers the train diff, so an unreviewed intermediate commit could bypass review and the owner sign-off path. Codex confirmed the defect with a bounded local reproducer; it was not exploited and nothing landed that way.
