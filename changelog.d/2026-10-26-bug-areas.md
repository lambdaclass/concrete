### Bug Areas Derived From The Records And Git History (2026-10-26)

Each numbered bug's compiler area (stage and module) and its found and fixed dates are now derived
from its fix commits, never recorded by hand: `scripts/gen/bug_areas.py` finds the commits that name
a bug, maps the paths they changed with the new `docs/bugs/area_map.tsv` and weights them by changed
lines in compiler, std and tooling code. `docs/bugs/AREAS.md` is the generated view (checked with the
other ledger views; it goes stale when a fix commit changes code, and one regeneration settles it),
and `scripts/gen/bug_ledger.py report` prints the research tables (`--json`, `--sqlite PATH`). A
record may carry an `area:` override where the record shows the derivation is wrong; seventeen do.
The ledger gate checks the mapping covers every fix-commit path, the report is deterministic, an
override naming an unknown stage is refused, a shallow clone is refused and a regeneration commit
is a fixed point. The CI jobs that run the docs-drift gate check out the full history.
