### Loops are `while` and a counted range loop (OD47, decided 2026-10-29)

The project owner decided that the C-style `for` is removed in every form and that the loops are
`while cond { ... }` and the counted loop `for i from a until b { ... }` (half-open, the index
immutable and of the bounds' integer type, the bounds evaluated once, `from` and `until` reserved),
which Core desugars to its one `while` form with the termination measure `b - i`. The specification
records it as STMT-6@3 and STMT-11@2 (pending); bug 331 implements it and migrates the corpus under
exit 2q, and the grammar change is on its own branch. Two questions it raises are open for the
owner: OD48 (the index type over two literal bounds) and OD49 (the measure's arithmetic).
