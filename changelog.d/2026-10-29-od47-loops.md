### Loops are `while` and a counted range loop (OD47, decided 2026-10-29)

The project owner decided that the C-style `for` is removed in every form and that the loops are
`while cond { ... }` and the counted loop `for i from a until b { ... }` (half-open, the index
immutable and scoped to the body, of the bounds' integer type, the bounds evaluated once left to
right, the comparison before the increment so nothing overflows at the type's maximum, `continue`
running the body's exit sequence and then advancing only its own loop), which Core desugars to its
one `while` form with the termination measure `b - i`, so a `#[variant]` on it is refused (STMT-12).
The specification records it as STMT-6@3, STMT-11@2 and STMT-12 (pending), with the review's
edge cases as examples; bug 331 implements it and migrates the corpus under exit 2q, and the grammar
change is on its own branch. Four questions are open for the owner: OD48 (the index type over two
literal bounds), OD49 (the measure's arithmetic), OD50 (reserved or contextual `from` and `until`)
and OD51 (reverse, stepped and collection iteration).
