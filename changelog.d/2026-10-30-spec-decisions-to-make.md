### Language specification: every open owner question in one ordered list (2026-10-30)

The simplification, raw-pointer and grammar questions of the 2026-10-30 owner picks are owner decisions
OD52 – OD91, each a ROADMAP row and an open entry (OPEN-52 to OPEN-91) checked by S8: the Austral-style
removals (S1 – S3, S7, S8, S10 – S12b, a coordinated removal list), Q3 and Q4, G19, the grammar items (P2
– P7, G1-b, the resync B, the eight constructs C1 – C8, R1, R2/R3, R8, R9, `Copy`, and the cast `x as T < y` of the LL(1) ledger) and the review's new
items (empty structs, `()`, SCOPE-4, failure in a deferred call, what "the remaining locals are
destroyed" means). `docs/spec/decisions.md` opens with all 60 open questions in reading order, each
with Austral's choice and, where given, the coordinator's pick (a recommendation, not a decision), the
PROJECT.md principle it rests on, its proof impact and what it would delete.
The review of the OD47 loop rules added OD104 (a `#[variant]` on a counted loop) and OD105 (where its
`#[invariant]` is checked), which STMT-12 had stated as rules; STMT-12 now states only OD47's desugaring.
