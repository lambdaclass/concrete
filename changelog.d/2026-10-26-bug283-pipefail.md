### Gates match text without a pipe into `grep -q` (bug 283)

Under `set -o pipefail`, `producer | grep -q PAT` let grep exit at its first match, the producer die
of SIGPIPE and the pipeline report 141: a positive leg failed with its pattern present, and a negated
leg passed with its forbidden text present. Gates now match with `has_match`
(`scripts/tests/lib/has_match.sh`, loaded by `lib/gate_env.sh`), which reads all of its input, keeps
a failing producer's status, and stops the script with no verdict on a grep error. Every site under
`scripts/` and the pre-push hook was rewritten with each leg's polarity unchanged, and
`check_gate_hygiene.sh` now refuses any pipe into a short-circuiting grep (`-q`, `-m`, `-l`, `-L`)
or through `head` into a match, proving the race and the fix on a large producer
([docs/bugs/283_gate_grep_q_sigpipe_false_fail.md](docs/bugs/283_gate_grep_q_sigpipe_false_fail.md)).
