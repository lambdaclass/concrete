### Cross-machine reproducibility includes concrete-ci-2 (ISS-0029) (2026-11-08)

The required Linux reproducibility legs are now `linux-github` on GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04` and
`linux-ci2` on the self-hosted `concrete` runner pool, with no GitHub-hosted fallback for the latter.
Each manifest records its runner environment, host and runner name. `compare-linux` verifies that it
has one GitHub-hosted and one concrete-ci-2 manifest, then compares all build-identity and artifact
SHA-256 lines. Missing manifests or hashes, wrong provenance and differing artifacts fail closed.
The workflow check parses these requirements and runs the extracted comparison step against passing
and failing fixture manifests.
