### Cross-machine reproducibility includes concrete-ci-2 (ISS-0029) (2026-11-08)

The required Linux reproducibility legs are now `linux-github` on GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04` and
`linux-ci2` on concrete-ci-2 for pushes to main and dispatch runs. The CI2 route requires the general
`concrete` pool label and the provisioned host-specific `concrete-ci-2` label, excluding the light
pool. On `pull_request`, `linux-ci2` selects a GitHub-hosted runner and fails in its first step
rather than running PR code on concrete-ci-2 or passing on a substitute.

Each manifest records `github.repository`, the runner environment and the runner name.
`compare-linux` requires matching repository provenance and an exact entry for that repository and
runner in `scripts/checks/lib/ci2_runners.txt`, not a name pattern, then compares all build-identity
and artifact SHA-256 lines. The inventory lists `gha1`–`gha3` for `unbalancedparentheses/concrete2`
and `gha4`–`gha6` for `lambdaclass/concrete`; `gha7`/`gha8` are excluded. Missing inventories,
manifests, repository provenance or hashes, unregistered or cross-repository runners, wrong
environments and differing artifacts fail closed. The documented trust boundary is operator-only
runner registration and the private dotfiles `servers/` provisioning, not a runner name alone.

The workflow check evaluates the extracted guard condition for hosted, self-hosted and substitute
contexts and executes its shell body to require a real refusal. Mutation controls prove that changing
the body to `exit 0 # exit 1`, inverting the condition, removing the guard or moving it after
checkout all fail. The extracted comparison step also runs against passing and failing fixtures.
