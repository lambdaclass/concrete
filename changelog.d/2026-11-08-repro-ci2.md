### Cross-machine reproducibility includes concrete-ci-2 (ISS-0029) (2026-11-08)

The required Linux reproducibility legs are now `linux-github` on GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04` and
`linux-ci2` on self-hosted concrete-ci-2 for pushes to main and dispatch runs. On `pull_request`,
`linux-ci2` selects a GitHub-hosted runner and fails in its first step rather than running PR code
on concrete-ci-2 or passing on a substitute. Each manifest records the runner-reported environment
and name; `compare-linux` requires GitHub-hosted provenance for one and self-hosted provenance with
a `concrete-ci-2-gha<N>` name for the other, then compares all build-identity and artifact SHA-256
lines. Missing manifests or hashes, wrong provenance and differing artifacts fail closed. The
workflow check parses the runner guard and runs the extracted comparison step against passing and
failing fixture manifests.
