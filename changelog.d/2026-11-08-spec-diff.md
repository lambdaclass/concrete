### Seeded spec/compiler differential

Two required CI jobs now run the seeded differential on x86-64 Linux and AArch64 macOS. The spec leg
checks generated, accepted slice-0 Core programs against the executable spec and exposes the fixed
`compileCore` lowering hook; it reports zero compiled programs until that hook is implemented. The
interim BIR leg compares BIR semantics with LLVM-native, LCB-native and LCB's in-process evaluator
through the shared observation judge. Both legs are deterministic and replayable by seed and index,
with construct/outcome coverage, bounded run time, regression instructions and planted-failure
controls.
