### Language specification, slices 4 and 5: ten more design documents absorbed or moved (2026-10-29)

CAPABILITY_FACTS (its rule into TRUST-4; the compiler record to `docs/compiler/`), FAILURE_STRATEGY
(into the statements chapter's "Abort and failure", now stating the decided exit sequence and no
write-back), STATEMENT_EXPRESSION_MODEL (STMT-4, STMT-8), PATTERN_ERGONOMICS (the `_` binder, PAT-5),
LANGUAGE_INVARIANTS (the new explanatory chapter `docs/spec/overview.md`),
VISIBILITY_AND_MODULE_HYGIENE (MOD-2) and OWNERSHIP_MODEL (the ownership chapter's introduction) were
absorbed, their design histories archived under `docs/archive/design-records/`; MEMORY_GUARANTEES and
SAFETY moved to `docs/verification/` and HANDLE_CAPABILITIES_AUDIT to `docs/stdlib/`.
