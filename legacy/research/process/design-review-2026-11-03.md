“Owner decision needed” above means a remaining observable language choice or approval of the proposed Core interface. “Engineering” means formalizing or implementing an already chosen requirement; it does not require reopening the authority design.

The deliberate chapter disagreements are generally **not independent contradictions**: `docs/spec/decisions.md:10–25` explicitly establishes precedence until Lean replacement. The research influences likewise supply techniques, not missing semantics—particularly functional translation, ownership-preserving compilation and open-module composition (`docs/project/INFLUENCES.md:525–589`).

**Verdict:** ready to **draft** the target Core proposal, but not ready to approve or freeze it. The proposal is the appropriate vehicle for resolving the remaining Core choices; requiring those choices to be settled before drafting would be circular. Before drafting, complete a concrete audit disposition and draft the contract/theorem boundaries. Slice 1 is a good bootstrap size once its exact support and assurance scopes are pinned down. A1–A3 contain most necessary obligations, but their current linear ordering and exits are insufficient.

Settle matters in this order:

1. **Theorem and slice boundary:** exact admitted subset, observations, accepted trap witness, proof endpoint and LLVM assumptions.
2. **Allocator discipline:** brands, generativity/reset, cross-function dependencies, escaping results, stable release state and conserving failure.
3. **Reference/resource interfaces:** heap opening and array views, raw-memory validity, destruction bounds and deferred-loan/cleanup order.
4. **Remaining observable choices:** static-text typing, ambiguous capability inference, mandatory static refusals and target-grant contracts.
5. **Whole Core proposal:** constructor/type/binder inventory, independent judgments, evaluator and instrumentation, elaboration, representation and module/interface boundaries.
6. **Feasibility evidence and queue closure:** representative design proofs before freeze; explicit A/B task splits, totality from B1, canonical decision references and a complete trusted-base inventory.
