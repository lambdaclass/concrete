**Findings**

1. **ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Adequacy.lean:181 — major — REAL DEFECT: the replacement oracle law still holds by construction.**

   `Move.toQueryOracle_answer` eliminates `Move.prim` and returns its constructor premises. `Move.prim` already requires the oracle answer, contract check, continuation result, and exact event append. Unfolding `toQueryOracle` supplies the remaining equality. An implementation that answers incorrectly cannot satisfy the premise, so this law cannot detect that implementation.

   `bumpedMove` supplies a satisfiable instance, but the occurrence-sensitive oracle example at line 1446 tests `runFromQ_toReport`, not this law: it changes the oracle and compares evaluator reports.

   **Concrete fix:** prove an evaluator-level request/answer correspondence from an actual issued query and the evaluator’s resulting report or execution segment. Its premise must not already assert the selected answer and resulting transition. Include successful, unanswered, invalid-answer, and refused-continuation cases. Plant an evaluator mutation that substitutes an answer or queries the wrong history, and demonstrate that the correspondence rejects it.

2. **ConcreteSpec/Spec/Adequacy.lean:254 — major — REAL DEFECT: termination certificates can disable every obligation.**

   `Premises := Empty` makes `terminates` unconditionally dischargeable. With inhabited premises, `admits := fun _ _ _ _ => False` does the same. Neither construction requires a real function, successful entry, or terminating execution. Both exported laws at `Proofs/Adequacy.lean:1055` and `:1071` then have unavailable admission hypotheses. There is no concrete `TerminationEvidence` witness in this module.

   **Concrete fix:** distinguish a conditional termination theorem from a usable certificate for a declared call domain. Fix that domain independently of the certificate, prove coverage of it, and carry an inhabited admitted-call witness where a usable certificate is claimed. Add a real function certificate and negative controls for an empty domain, missing function, failed entry, and admitted spinning call. Do not require all conditional theorems to have inhabitants; prevent empty conditional theorems from masquerading as usable evidence.

3. **ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Adequacy.lean:756,760 — major — REAL DEFECT under the supplied vacuity rule: the round-trip laws are construction identities.**

   Both conversions retain exactly the supplied `states` function. The round-trip equalities are `rfl`; proof irrelevance removes differences between their proof fields. Consequently these equalities cannot independently detect an incorrect execution correspondence. The substantive correspondence is established in the conversion constructors through evaluator-prefix proofs, not by these round-trip laws.

   **Concrete fix:** identify the constructor proofs and pointwise evaluator/transition correspondence as the adequacy obligations, and provide positive and planted negative controls for those obligations. Keep the round trips explicitly classified as representation helpers, without counting them as independent contract evidence.
