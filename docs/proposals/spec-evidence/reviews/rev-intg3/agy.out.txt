### Findings

1. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Adequacy.lean:881-969` & `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Adequacy.lean:238-243` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] `finite_projected_observation` and `infinite_projected_observation` discharge `Observe.HasFullObservation` by `SameInfiniteObservation.refl _` via existential choice of `steps`**
   - **Problem:** Both theorems state `∃ steps ..., MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps ∧ Observe.HasFullObservation (.infinite steps) ...` and instantiate `steps` to the canonical C6 target (`Observe.finiteDivergenceSteps events` at line 895 and `fun i => .emit (trace i)` at line 960). Consequently, the entire C6 observation obligation `Observe.HasFullObservation` is proved by `Observe.SameInfiniteObservation.refl _` (lines 896 and 961). The theorems never exercise `SameInfiniteObservation` across non-identical step sequences and never prove that *any* C6 step sequence matching `(A, X)` has observation `.finiteDivergence events` / `.infiniteDivergence trace`.
   - **Concrete fix:**
     1. Prove that `MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps` characterizes the C6 observation for **every** matching `steps`:
        ```lean
        theorem MatchesProjectedHistory.hasFullObservation_finite
            (hmatch : MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps)
            (hstable : ∀ n, start ≤ n → A.project (X.states n).events = events) :
            Observe.HasFullObservation (.infinite steps) (.finiteDivergence events)

        theorem MatchesProjectedHistory.hasFullObservation_infinite
            (hmatch : MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps)
            (htrace : ∀ i n, i < (A.project (X.states n).events).length →
              (A.project (X.states n).events)[i]? = some (trace i)) :
            Observe.HasFullObservation (.infinite steps) (.infiniteDivergence trace)
        ```
     2. Strengthen `finite_projected_observation` and `infinite_projected_observation` so they provide both existence of a matching C6 step stream and soundness for **all** `steps` satisfying `MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps` (i.e., `∃ events, (∃ steps, MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps) ∧ ∀ steps, MatchesProjectedHistory A X steps → Observe.HasFullObservation (.infinite steps) (.finiteDivergence events)`, and analogously for `infinite_projected_observation`).

2. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Adequacy.lean:125-136` — [major] [REAL DEFECT] `runFromQ_consecutive_other_site` does not prove counter invariance across interleaved sites**
   - **Problem:** The docstring claims *"A query at another site does not advance the current query's occurrence count, even when the requests are consecutive in an actual run"*, but the conclusion is only:
     `q'.occurrence = occurrenceAt ((runFromQ P ρ fuel events issued c).queries.take i) q'.site`
     Because `q'` is at index `i + 1`, `runFromQ_queryHistoryValid` already gives `q'.occurrence = occurrenceAt (queries.take (i + 1)) q'.site`. The theorem merely strips the single element `queries[i]` from `occurrenceAt` rather than relating two queries at the *same* site separated by queries at other sites. In addition, there is no top-level `runQ_queryHistoryValid` corollary for `issued = []`, forcing callers (lines 1381, 1391) to prove `QueryHistoryValid []` inline.
   - **Concrete fix:**
     1. Add `theorem QueryHistoryValid.nil : QueryHistoryValid ([] : List (Query O))` and `theorem runQ_queryHistoryValid : QueryHistoryValid (runQ P ρ fuel c).queries`.
     2. Prove the true interleaved-site law between two queries `q` (at index `i`) and `q'` (at index `j`, `i < j`) at the same site (`q.site = q'.site`) when all intervening queries `k` (`i < k < j`) have `queries[k].site ≠ q.site`: then `q'.occurrence = q.occurrence + 1` (and more generally, for any `i ≤ j`, `q'.occurrence = q.occurrence + count of intervening queries at q.site`).

3. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Adequacy.lean:181-194` — [minor] [REAL DEFECT] `Move.toQueryOracle_answer` drops the query-history component of `Position` and only states one direction**
   - **Problem:** `Move.toQueryOracle_answer` inverts `Move P ρ₀.toQueryOracle pos pos'` on a `.request` step, but omits `pos'.queries = pos.queries ++ [Query.issue pos.events p vs site st pos.queries]` from its conclusion and does not state the converse construction (or an `iff`) characterizing primitive moves under `ρ₀.toQueryOracle`.
   - **Concrete fix:** Upgrade `Move.toQueryOracle_answer` to an `iff` (or pair with its constructor direction) and include `pos'.queries = pos.queries ++ [Query.issue pos.events p vs site st pos.queries]` in the characterization of `pos'`.
