This audit is NOT a re-review of each part. Its job is the semantic consistency of the parts now that they are combined:
1. ONE OWNER PER DEFINITION. Each concept (span, source path, site table, trap line, exit status, ending or final result, store, event, step, environment, program, loader) has exactly one normative definition. Every other module references it. Report any concept defined twice, by a parallel structure, a copied predicate, a second validity condition, or a restated fact that could drift. Legitimate separate models (for example a base toy model's own Stmt or Ctx inside its namespace) are not duplicates unless they state a fact the core also states, with no link between the two.
2. NO DUPLICATE FACTS. In particular: a single SiteTable validity; a single Span with its offset and line or column facts; a single trap-line format and its exit statuses (125 and 134); a single definition of an ending or final result with its store.
3. NO WEAKENED LAW. Where one part's law depends on another part's definition, check that the merged definitions still give each law its intended meaning. Look for laws that became trivially true, vacuous or weaker because a definition they use changed in another part (for example a premise now always false, or a conclusion that no longer pins what it used to).
4. NO CONTRADICTION between parts: two definitions or laws that cannot both hold for the same program, or that give the same input different meanings.
