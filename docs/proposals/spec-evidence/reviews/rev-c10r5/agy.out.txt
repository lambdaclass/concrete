### Executive Summary

1. **Are the fixes real?**
   - **Partially, with major defects remaining:**
     - **`c1_call_frame` has an unsatisfiable premise (`C1EffectImplementation`) for any C1 program that performs C4 byte stores or allocations**: `C1EffectImplementation` (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:272-276`) quantifies over *all* arbitrary configurations `before : Exec.Config O` without assuming `(project before.store).memory.WellFormed tgt` or reachability from `entry`. Because `C4Effects.store` and `C4Effects.alloc` require `st.memory.WellFormed tgt`, while `Memory.store` in `storingOperands` succeeds whenever the target block is live and writable even if another block or `nextBlock` violates `WellFormed`, no `C4Effects` derivation exists from such a `before.store`. Consequently, `C1EffectImplementation` is **false (vacuous)** for `storingProgram`. This was hidden because the `Instance` for `c1_call_frame` (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:754-756`) is just a partial application `c1_call_frame linuxX8664` that leaves the universal implication unapplied, and a separate one-off theorem `storingCall_frame` was written using 4 fuel steps (`runStoringCall`) instead of instantiating `c1_call_frame`.
     - **Premises that switch off obligations**:
       - `transferKept_sound` (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:1207-1211`) adds `(tracked : before = .owned pair.handle ∨ before = .kept pair.handle)`, which switches off untracked handles `h ≠ pair.handle`. In fact, `transferKept` (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:652-656`) ignores `pair.handle` and emits `some (.call pair.submit h, .kept h)` for *any* `h`, which violates `KeptStep` whenever `h ≠ pair.handle`.
       - `toyLaunch_conforms` (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:1321-1322`) adds `(adequate : 8 ≤ reserved)`, switching off the Q8.5 under-reservation refusal branch (`reserved < 8 → .refused125`).
     - **`| none => True` and `| .refused125 => True` result wrappers weaken statements**: `EffectResult`, `CallFrameResult`, `TransferCorrect`, `CheckedPairCorrect`, and `ToyLaunchCorrect` map failure/refusal to `True`. As a result, a launcher that *always* returns `.refused125` (even when `8 ≤ reserved`) satisfies `ToyLaunchCorrect` and `toyLaunch_conforms`; a no-op or stuck `runStoringCall` satisfies `CallFrameResult`; and a constant-`none` `checkKeptPair` or `transferKept` satisfies `CheckedPairCorrect` / `TransferCorrect`.
     - **`C1CallContract` still smuggles the contract as a record field**: `structure C1CallContract` (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:287`) was left in the spec with `frame : ∀ {entry exit}, C1Call O f P entry exit → footprint.Preserves ...` as a structure field.
2. **Are the toy machines independent of what they check?**
   - `checkKeptPair` (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:635-642`) is a direct runtime `if`-recheck of `KeptPair.WellFormed` (`submit = complete` and `h = a ∧ h = b ∧ h = c`), returning `none` (mapped to `True` by `CheckedPairCorrect`) when it fails.

3. **Do the 54 helpers hide contract laws?**
   - **Yes.** Out of 54 `Helper` theorems, **29 are completely unused by any theorem in the file**. They include:
     - 12 record-projection and `rfl` theorems from Round 4 (`clobbers_only_changed_registers`, `caller_stack_slots_equal`, `stack_pointer_restored`, `typed_arguments_at_entry`, `typed_result_at_return`, `frame_size_aligned`, `frame_within_stack_bound`, `call_stack_account`, `artifact_stack_account`, `callee_saved_registers_equal`, `launcher_entry_exit`, `launcher_run_admitted`) relabeled `Helper (projection)` to bypass the ban on projection laws;

     - 6 retention laws (`extern_result_rejects_unrelated_retention`, `keptPair_results_valid`, `kept_only_paired_completion`, `submit_produces_paired_receipt`, `kept_receipt_rejects_submission`, `kept_receipt_rejects_other_extern`) and `stack_under_reservation` demoted to unused `Helper (computation)` lemmas.
4. **Are the named obligations legitimate deferrals?**
   - Deferring native x86-64 codegen (`Signature.WellFormed`, `TransitionConforms` at R-0580; `ArtifactStackConforms` at R-0575; `RetentionConforms` at slice 25, R-0580) and native ELF/syscall launcher integration (`LauncherConforms` at R-0503/R-0505/R-0584) is legitimate in scope, **except** that `ArtifactStackConforms`, `RetentionConforms`, and `LauncherConforms` are never even instantiated on the toy models in `Proofs/Frame.lean` (which instead define parallel wrapper predicates `StackSafe`, `TransferCorrect`, and `ToyLaunchCorrect`).
