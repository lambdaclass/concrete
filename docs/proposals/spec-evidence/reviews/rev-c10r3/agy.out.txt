6. **`c1Call_restores_caller`** (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:359-365`) and **`C1Call`** (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:127-142`)
   - **Verdict**: **VACUOUS / REDUNDANT HYPOTHESIS (REAL DEFECT — Minor)** on `exit.ctrl = .done ∧ exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller ∧ exit.frames = k`.
   - **Analysis**: `C1Call` now includes the explicit final step hypothesis `hr : Exec.step P ⟨.exiting (.ret result), callee, stReturn, .ret dst caller :: k⟩ = some (.next exit)`. By the definition of `Exec.step` on `.exiting (.ret result)` with frame `.ret dst caller :: k`, `exit` is uniquely determined (once `O.life (.leave callee) stReturn` succeeds) to have `exit.ctrl = .done`, `exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller`, and `exit.frames = k`. However, `C1Call` still keeps `hxc : exit.ctrl = .done`, `hxl : exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller`, and `hxf : exit.frames = k` as explicit conjuncts in the definition of `C1Call`, and `c1Call_restores_caller` simply projects `⟨dst, result, caller, k, hxl, hxf⟩` without ever using `hr`! Under the owner's binding vacuity rule ("a theorem that restates its definition"), `c1Call_restores_caller` is currently a direct projection of the conjuncts `hxl` and `hxf` baked into `C1Call`, rather than deriving them from the final `Exec.step` transition `hr`.
   - **Non-trivial instance**: `c1Entry` calling `c1FnId` ("seven") stepping to `c1Boundary` and then `c1Exit` (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:398-405`).
   - **Planted counter-example**: `⟨.done, [], (), [.ret none []]⟩` (unpopped `.ret` frame) is rejected (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:408-415`).

7. **`KeptPair.WellFormed`** (`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:356-361`)

### Findings

1. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:138-142` and `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:359-365` — [major] [REAL DEFECT]**
   - **Problem**: `C1Call` retains the redundant conjuncts `exit.ctrl = .done ∧ exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller ∧ exit.frames = k` alongside `Exec.step P ⟨.exiting (.ret result), callee, stReturn, .ret dst caller :: k⟩ = some (.next exit)`, and `c1Call_restores_caller` merely projects `hxl` and `hxf` directly out of `C1Call` without inspecting the final step `hr`. This violates the vacuity rule ("a theorem that restates its definition").
   - **Concrete fix**: Remove `exit.ctrl = .done ∧ exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller ∧ exit.frames = k` from `C1Call` in `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:138`, so the final configuration `exit` is constrained solely by `Exec.step P ⟨.exiting (.ret result), callee, stReturn, .ret dst caller :: k⟩ = some (.next exit)`. Then in `c1Call_restores_caller` (`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Frame.lean:359-365`), unfold `Exec.step` in `hr` (case-splitting on `O.life (.leave callee) stReturn`) to genuinely derive `exit.ctrl = .done`, `exit.locals = Exec.bindDst dst result caller`, and `exit.frames = k` from the operational semantics of the return step.

2. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Frame.lean:359-361` — [nit] [PREFERENCE]**
   - **Problem**: In `KeptPair.WellFormed`, once `submitResult = .receipt pair.handle` and `completeResult = .owned pair.handle` are asserted, the conjuncts `ExternResult.Valid pair pair.submit submitResult` and `ExternResult.Valid pair pair.complete completeResult` hold by `⟨rfl, rfl⟩` after substitution.
   - **Concrete fix**: Either drop the two redundant `ExternResult.Valid` conjuncts from `KeptPair.WellFormed` and prove a small lemma that `KeptPair.WellFormed` implies `ExternResult.Valid` for both endpoints, or keep as-is if intentional for self-documenting structure.
