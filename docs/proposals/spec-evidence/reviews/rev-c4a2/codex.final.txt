2. **major — REAL DEFECT — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean:600`: `access_valid` retains a guarded projection case.**  
   The zero-size branch returns `hv.2.2`, the validity check performed inside `Memory.access`. That part of the claimed validity law is true because the operation rechecks its conclusion. This violates the binding runtime-recheck rule and the final projection rule.  
   **Fix:** make the independently derived law explicitly nonempty (`n ≠ 0`), and classify the zero-size validity extraction as a Helper. Keep zero-size operational behavior covered separately by substantive load/store laws.

3. **major — REAL DEFECT — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Hybrid.lean:815`, `:826`, `:839`, `:852`: all four frame Instances observe an absent place.**  
   Place `1` exists in neither fixture. Consequently:
   - `write_frame` and `write_frame_disjoint` observe `none = none`;
   - `materialize_frame` and `expose_frame` also observe `none = none`;
   - `before_sep` and `before_disjoint` explicitly discharge separation through absence.

   The planted readers distinguish global memory changes even for an absent place. Their types match the convention, but these witnesses avoid exercising preservation of another stored value.  
   **Fix:** use two existing materialized places backed by distinct live allocations, with a successful decode at the observed place. For materialization/exposure, retain that second place through the transition. Plant a reader or operation defect that corrupts that actual observation.

4. **major — REAL DEFECT — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean:111`, `:345`: reflexivity computations remain classified as Laws.**  
   `decodeBool_encodeBool` is `cases b <;> rfl`. `eqAddr_provenance` unfolds address equality and proves equality of identical address/space components. Planted mutants exist, but the convention separately prohibits reflexivity Laws; mutants do not waive that check.  
   **Fix:** reclassify these computational lemmas, and provide semantic contract coverage through independently stated boolean-representation and pointer-comparison laws. Pointer comparison coverage should characterize differing addresses/spaces as well as provenance independence.

5. **major — REAL DEFECT — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean:323`, `:457`, `:821`, `:848`, `:865`: semantic guarantees are hidden behind Helper exemptions.**  
   Retaining released identities, reconstruction soundness, exposure framing, and preservation of block geometry are externally meaningful C4 guarantees. Existing well-formedness or read-preservation laws do not imply them: a mutant can change geometry while preserving well-formedness, or change exposed reconstruction while retaining completeness on the currently tested cases.  
   **Fix:** retain implementation decomposition helpers where useful, but expose these guarantees as contract Laws with full closed Instances and premise-preserving Planted examples. I do not approve these exemptions.
