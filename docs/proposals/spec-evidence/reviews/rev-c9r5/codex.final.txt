All findings below are **REAL DEFECTS**, not preferences.

1. **Major — `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Ident.lean:502`: the new function-table assignment omits every nested construct.**

   `collectKeysIn` emits `construct.key scope` and recurses only into `rest`; it never visits `construct.children`. Consequently, `assignFunctionTable` assigns no sites or complete persistent paths to nested operations. For example, collecting `[nestedBody]` yields only the outer block key, omitting its loop and primitive.

   The outside-edit theorem is mathematically meaningful, but proves preservation for an incomplete assignment.

   **Concrete fix:** implement recursive traversal that emits every site-bearing occurrence’s complete path, using the same scope and prefix semantics as `pathAtIn`. Prove correspondence between collection, occurrence lookup, and assigned site tables. Include a nested-body instance and a mutant that drops descendants.

2. **Major — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Ident.lean:152`, `:160`, `:343`, `:386`: definitional equations are classified as Laws.**

   These are respectively `key_rename_named`, `key_pin_unnamed`, `descendant_named_ancestor_resets_scope`, and `descendant_rename_named`. Unfolding the named operations reduces their two sides to the same expression. Their proofs merely expose the definitions; adding mutants does not cure the convention’s prohibition on definitional Laws.

   **Concrete fix:** classify these as Representation/computation lemmas, explicitly excluding them from soundness evidence. Establish the required rename, pinning, and named-scope contracts over actual occurrence assignment and edits, with correspondence to complete assigned tables.

4. **Major — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Prim.lean:283`: `Ghost.drive_steps` is a contract law disguised as a computation helper.**

   **Concrete fix:** classify `drive_steps` as a Law with matching closed Instance/Planted evidence, or perform the induction directly in the public soundness law and remove this exemption.

5. **Major — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Observe2.lean:410`: the new location Law repackages the validity premise.**

This fails FINAL’s prohibition on projection Laws. The connection is useful, but its present form is support plumbing rather than independent contract evidence.

   **Concrete fix:** retain this as an inversion/helper lemma; prove a substantive observation contract over the produced terminal/rendered result, with an independently stated source-location specification and a matching mutant at unchanged inputs.

6. **Minor — `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Observe2.lean:483`, `:492`: C9’s existing byte-column theorems remain unclassified and lack FINAL evidence.**

   **Concrete fix:** classify the rendering substitution as a helper/representation lemma; classify and witness `byteColumn_tab`. Do not grandfather these C9 laws because they predate this delta.
