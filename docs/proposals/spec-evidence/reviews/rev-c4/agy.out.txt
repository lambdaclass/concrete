1. `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean:190` (and `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Memory.lean:115`) — **major** (`REAL DEFECT`): `Memory.WellFormed` was strengthened with `canonical` and `unique` (whose doc comment at `Spec/Memory.lean:115` states *"so first-entry updates preserve canonicality"*), yet only `alloc_wellFormed` is proved. There are no `WellFormed` preservation theorems for `Memory.modify`, `Memory.store`, `Memory.free`, `Memory.expose`, or `Memory.empty`. Because `store_load`, `store_same_block_frame`, `write_read`, `fromExposed_sound`, and `alloc_ne_existing` all require `m.WellFormed tgt`, callers cannot chain `Memory.empty`, `State.materialize` (which runs `alloc` then `store`), `State.write`, `State.rawWrite`, `State.expose`, or `Memory.free` while maintaining the `WellFormed` invariant needed by subsequent steps.
   - **Fix**: Prove `empty_wellFormed`, a helper `modify_wellFormed` for in-place block updates that preserve geometry (`space`, `base`, `size`, `align`, `bytes.length`) and do not mark dead blocks live, and derive `store_wellFormed`, `free_wellFormed`, and `expose_wellFormed` in `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean` (plus `materialize_wellFormed` and `write_wellFormed` in `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Hybrid.lean`).

2. `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Hybrid.lean:96` — **major** (`REAL DEFECT`): `materialize_projection`, `materialize_reuse`, and `materialize_idempotent` only inspect the `places` map and never read back from the materialized byte range. There is no theorem showing that first materialization of a structured place preserves its value when read back (`st.read tgt p = some v → st.materialize tgt p s base align padding = some (r, st') → st'.read tgt p = some v`) or that `materialize` and `write` frame other places (`q ≠ p`).
   - **Fix**: In `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Hybrid.lean`, prove that reading `p` after `st.materialize tgt p s base align padding = some (r, st')` on a `WellFormed` memory returns the original value `v` (using `alloc_wellFormed`, `store_load` or a prefix load lemma showing `((encode tgt v ++ padding).take (sizeof v.ty)) = encode tgt v`, and `rep_roundtrip`), and add frame lemmas for `q ≠ p`.

3. `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean:267` (and `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Hybrid.lean:147`) — **minor** (`REAL DEFECT`): Decided rows Q3.9 and Q3.10 specify that `expose` records the block as exposed and `from_exposed` picks in-bounds, then one-past, else no provenance. Neither `Proofs/Memory.lean` nor `Proofs/Hybrid.lean` contains any theorem about `Memory.expose` or `State.expose`, and `fromExposed_sound` only covers the `(m.fromExposed s addr).prov = some b` implication without the `none` case or the existence/completeness direction (that an eligible containing block guarantees `(m.fromExposed s addr).prov = some b` for that unique block).
   - **Fix**: Add `expose_spec` in `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Memory.lean` (characterizing `m.expose p = some m'`), `fromExposed_none` and `fromExposed_contains` (using `candidate_contains_unique`), and an `expose_projection`/`expose_candidate` lemma in `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Hybrid.lean`.

4. `ConcreteSpec/Spec/Hybrid.lean:28` — **minor** (`REAL DEFECT`): `Hybrid.Ty` and `Hybrid.Value` include only `int` and `bool`, omitting scalar pointers (`Ptr`), even though `Spec/Memory.lean` and `Proofs/Memory.lean` already define `encodePtr`, `decodePtr`, and `decodePtr_encodePtr` without deciding the open partial-pointer question. Without a `.ptr` case in `Hybrid.Ty` / `Hybrid.Value`, places in `Hybrid.State` cannot store the pointers returned by `materialize`/`expose`, and C1/C5 will have to change `sizeof`'s signature to take `tgt : Target`.
   - **Fix**: Add `| ptr` to `Hybrid.Ty` and `| ptr (p : Ptr)` to `Hybrid.Value` (threading `tgt : Target` into `sizeof`, delegating `encode`/`decode` to `encodePtr`/`decodePtr`, and using `decodePtr_encodePtr` in `rep_roundtrip` under `tgt.WellFormed`), or at minimum make `sizeof` take `tgt : Target` now and document in `Spec/Hybrid.lean` where pointer-typed places are added.
