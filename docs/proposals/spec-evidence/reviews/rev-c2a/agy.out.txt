### 2. Findings

1. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Const.lean:397-412` & `ConcreteSpec/README.md:59-61` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] Item 19's two global theorems (`DependencySortingComplete` and `ConstOrderIndependent`) are left as unproved `def ... : Prop` obligations.**
   - **Problem:** Item 19 is decided for C2 in this round. Encoding `DependencySortingComplete` and `ConstOrderIndependent` as unproved `Prop` definitions in `Spec/Const.lean` defers the core metatheory of forward references to A2i.
   - **Concrete fix:** Prove both as theorems in `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Const.lean` (with `Law` tags, closed `Instance`s, and `Planted` counterexamples) and remove the deferred-obligation note from `ConcreteSpec/README.md`.

2. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Const.lean:230-363, 610-616` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] No law governs `CExpr.eval` or `evalConsts` on the new fallible fragment when evaluation fails or runs with sufficient fuel (`e.fallibleFree = true` switches off Item 18).**
   - **Problem:** `eval_total` adds the premise `e.fallibleFree = true`, excluding `.plain`, `.plainNeg`, and `.tryConvert`. `evalConsts_sound` assumes `ConstsAdmitted`, which assumes `(evalConsts U fuel ds []).isOk = true`. `typed_not_admitted_iff_refused_proj` is a trivial boolean projection that does not even rule out `.error (.invalid _ _)` on well-typed declarations. Consequently, `plain_not_stuck` is never used in `Proofs/Const.lean`, `plainNeg_not_stuck` is missing from `Proofs/Scalar.lean`, and a mutant `CExpr.eval` that returns `.invalid` or `.outOfFuel` on `1 / 0` still satisfies every theorem in `Proofs/Const.lean`.
   - **Concrete fix:** Add `plainNeg_not_stuck` to `Proofs/Scalar.lean`, and prove a `Law` in `Proofs/Const.lean` (e.g., `eval_wellTyped_progress` / `typed_evalConsts_refusal`) showing that for `e.ty? Γ = some T`, `Agree env Γ`, and `e.height ≤ fuel`, `e.eval env fuel` is never `.invalid` and never `.outOfFuel`—it is either `.value v` (with `v.ty = T ∧ v.Valid`) or `.failure f` for a failing fallible operation in `e`—and hence for `ConstsTyped U ds` with sufficient fuel, `evalConsts U fuel ds []` is either `.ok env` or `.error (.operation id f)` with `id ∈ ds.map ConstDecl.id`.

3. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Const.lean:406-412` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] `ConstOrderIndependent` assumes both permutations are admitted, switching off order-independence of admission.**
   - **Problem:** `ConstOrderIndependent` takes both `ConstsAdmitted U fuel first` and `ConstsAdmitted U fuel second` as premises. If one topological sort of `source` were admitted and another refused, the implication would hold vacuously.
   - **Concrete fix:** State and prove that for `DependencyOrder U source first` and `DependencyOrder U source second`:
     1. `ConstsTyped U first ↔ ConstsTyped U second` (with `∀ c, AList.lookup Γ₁ c = AList.lookup Γ₂ c`),
     2. `ConstsEvaluate U fuel first ↔ ConstsEvaluate U fuel second` (and when `.ok env₁` and `.ok env₂`, `∀ c, AList.lookup env₁ c = AList.lookup env₂ c`),
     3. Hence `ConstsAdmitted U fuel first ↔ ConstsAdmitted U fuel second`.

4. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Const.lean:388-395` & `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Const.lean:544-605` — [major] [REAL DEFECT] `cycle_chain_real` is a `Bool`/`Prop` reflection helper, and no law proves that a `CycleChain` precludes a `DependencyOrder`.**
   - **Problem:** `ActualRefChain` (`Spec/Const.lean:363-366`) is an exact structural copy of `refChain` (`Spec/Const.lean:348-351`) in `Prop`, so `cycle_chain_real` only reflects `&&`/`List.any` into `∧`/`∃`. Meanwhile, `ElaboratedConsts` (`Spec/Const.lean:393-395`) pairs `DependencyOrder` and `CycleChain` without proving that they are mutually exclusive (`CycleChain U source chain → ∀ order, ¬ DependencyOrder U source order`).
   - **Concrete fix:** Reclassify `cycle_chain_real` as a `Representation` helper, and prove the semantic law `cycle_excludes_dependencyOrder : CycleChain U source chain → ∀ order, ¬ DependencyOrder U source order` (equivalently `DependencyOrder U source order → Acyclic U source`) with an `Instance` and a `Planted` mutant.

5. **`ConcreteSpec/Spec/Const.lean:261-267, 302, 436-444, 509-512` & `ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Const.lean:480-540` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] `admitted_copy` still depends on the runtime `U.isCopy d.ty = true` guard in `typeConsts`, while `ofC_copy` was dropped from `ConstTypes` so `NonCopyU` could treat `()` as non-`Copy`.**
   - **Problem:** In Concrete, every scalar in `CTy` (`IntTy`, `bool`, `unit`) is `Copy`. Removing `ofC_copy : ∀ c, isCopy (ofC c) = true` from `ConstTypes` allowed defining `NonCopyU` where `ofC .unit = .text` (`isCopy .text = false`), which falsifies `admitted_copy` only by claiming `()` has a non-`Copy` type. Meanwhile, `typeConsts` still has the explicit runtime guard `U.isCopy d.ty = true` (`Spec/Const.lean:302`), and `admitted_copy` (`Proofs/Const.lean:501-509`) merely rewrites `admitted_copy_proj` (that guard) along `U.ofC v.ty = d.ty`.
   - **Concrete fix:**
     1. Restore `ofC_copy : ∀ c, isCopy (ofC c) = true` to `ConstTypes` (and remove `NonCopyU` and `ofC_copy_proj`).
     2. Remove the redundant `U.isCopy d.ty = true` check from `typeConsts` (`Spec/Const.lean:302`), leaving `if U.ofC T = d.ty ∧ AList.lookup Γ d.id = none`.
     3. Prove `admitted_copy` (for `d.ty` and `U.ofC v.ty`) from `U.ofC T = d.ty` and `U.ofC_copy`—with zero runtime `isCopy` check in `typeConsts` or `evalConsts`.
     4. Use a mutant of `typeConsts` that drops `U.ofC T = d.ty` on `[⟨cid "S", .text, .unit⟩]` in `ConstDemo.U` for the `Planted` counterexample, and keep `¬ ConstsAdmitted U 20 [⟨cid "S", .text, .unit⟩]` in `ConstDemo.U`.

6. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Scalar.lean:37-360, 447-534` — [blocker] [REAL DEFECT] Blanket tagging of all 38 pre-existing theorems in `Proofs/Scalar.lean` as `Helper (...) (approved: PENDING)`.**
   - **Problem:** To satisfy tag checks while editing `checkedNeg`, all 38 pre-existing theorems in `Proofs/Scalar.lean`—including C1's headline contract laws (`eval_toExact`, `machine_plain_eq_exact`, `toBits_shl`, `toBits_shr_unsigned`, `toBits_bitwise`, `toBits_bitNot`, `checked_inRange`, `lossless_iff`, `saturate_below`, `saturate_above`, etc.)—were tagged `Helper (arithmetic)` / `Helper (inversion)` with `(approved: PENDING)`. Many of these theorems are never used in `Proofs/Const.lean`; they are C1's contract laws.
   - **Concrete fix:** Do not classify C1's contract laws as `Helper`s. Either scope the C2 branch diff in `Proofs/Scalar.lean` strictly to `checkedNeg` (`neg_inRange_iff`, `checkedNeg_ok_arithmetic`, `checkedNeg_error_iff`, plus `plainNeg_not_stuck`), or if `Proofs/Scalar.lean` is being audited under the convention in this change, tag C1's contract laws as `Law` with `Instance` and `Planted` witnesses and remove/demote the vacuous C1 restatements listed in Section 3.

7. **`ConcreteSpec/Proofs/Const.lean:440-452, 480-498, 501-509, 610-616` — [minor] [REAL DEFECT] Redundant conclusion conjunct in `evalConsts_sound` / `admitted_copy` and dead `_proj` lemmas.**
   - **Problem:**
     - `evalConsts_sound` and `admitted_copy` assume `h : ConstsAdmitted U fuel ds` (whose second conjunct is `(evalConsts U fuel ds []).isOk = true`) and conclude `∃ env, evalConsts U fuel ds [] = .ok env ∧ ...`, repeating the hypothesis in the conclusion and placing the subject `evalConsts` in both hypothesis and conclusion.
     - `admitted_iff_typed_evaluates_proj` (`Iff.rfl`), `ofC_copy_proj`, and `typed_not_admitted_iff_refused_proj` have zero callers in `Proofs/Const.lean`.
   - **Concrete fix:** State `evalConsts_sound` with hypotheses `(ht : ConstsTyped U ds) (he : evalConsts U fuel ds [] = .ok env)` (promoting `evalConsts_sound_inv`) or conclude `∀ env, evalConsts U fuel ds [] = .ok env → ...`; delete unused `_proj` restatements.
