1. **ConcreteSpec/Spec/Own.lean:106 — major — REAL DEFECT: frame ownership is still incomplete.**

   The new invariant says that slots belonging to an active frame appear in its locals. It does **not** say that every slot belongs to an active frame, as ROUND6 explicitly requires.

   For example, start from `Examples.initial`, replace its frames with `[⟨1, [], []⟩]`, and set `nextActivation := 2`. Its activation-0 linear slot is orphaned, yet the new `WellFormed` accepts it. `leave` successfully pops frame 1 and leaves that obligation behind.

   The locals index also lacks the reverse ownership relationship: a callee’s locals can name a caller’s slot. With a Copy caller slot, a well-formed callee containing that alias can successfully remove the caller’s slot during `leave`.

   **Long-term fix:** make the slot/frame/local relationship complete and sound in both directions: every slot belongs to an active frame, every slot is indexed by its owning frame, and local entries cannot designate another activation’s slots. Prove preservation and cleanup from that relationship. Do not restore a runtime scan.
