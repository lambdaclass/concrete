## Cause

Two mechanisms (Codex review, 2026-10-26):

- `Check/CheckHelpers.lean`: `blockDiverges`, `blockNonTerminating` and `blockExitsFunction`
  look at a block's last statement and recurse through an `if` with an `else` (both branches) and
  a `match` (every arm). `Check.lean` asks them of each branch of every statement `if`/`else`
  (and of every arm of every `match`, and through `exemptExitBranch`) as it checks it, so a nest's
  facts were recomputed from the bottom at each of its n levels.
- `Check.lean`, `specBindersStmt` / `specBindersArm` / `specBinders` (the names bound anywhere in
  a function body, collected for its loop contracts on every function): each level concatenated
  its nested blocks' name lists into its own (`flatMap`, `++`), so a binding n blocks deep was
  copied once per enclosing level. A wildcard arm binds `_`, so a `match` nest has both costs.

## Fix

- `CheckHelpers.blockEnds` computes the three facts of a block in one walk, as a tree mirroring
  the predicates' own recursion (the branches of an `if` with an `else`, the arms of a `match`,
  through parentheses; a leaf asks the original predicates of a statement they do not recurse
  through), so `(blockEnds b).facts.diverges = blockDiverges b` and likewise for the other two by
  construction. `checkStmt`, `checkStmts`, `checkExpr` and `checkExprCore` take the tree of what
  they check as an optional argument (as bug 280's `flex`): a statement `if` and a `match` use
  their kids for their branches and hand each branch block its subtree, which `checkStmtsCore`
  hands its last statement. A construct handed no tree, or one of another shape
  (`EndsTree.kidsIf`), computes its own, so no site walks more than it did before.
- The binder collector pushes onto one accumulator (`specBindersAcc` and its statement and arm
  forms), in the order the list version produced.

`--emit-ssa` and `--emit-llvm` (stdout, stderr with every diagnostic, and exit status) of every
`.con` under `tests/programs` and `examples` (4122 compiles), 3000 programs of
`fuzz_linearity.py`'s generator (accepted and refused) and 2000 of `fuzz_differential.py`'s are
